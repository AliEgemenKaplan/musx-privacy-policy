MusX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

MusX ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the MusX mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

MusX does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, address, or phone number in order to use the application.

The application may store certain information locally on your device, such as downloaded or saved music, playback preferences, and application settings. This information is primarily used to provide the application's features and is stored on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

MusX may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, including advertising and technical services.

Third-party service providers may collect information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostics, and usage information according to their own privacy policies.

For example, MusX may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and applicable laws.

3. Advertising

MusX may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising providers. These providers may use advertising identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

You can learn more about how Google handles information through Google's own privacy and advertising policies.

4. Music and External Content

MusX may provide functionality related to accessing, streaming, or saving publicly available media content from external services. MusX does not claim ownership of third-party content.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of external content complies with applicable laws and the terms of the relevant services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

MusX is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because MusX does not require users to create an account, we do not maintain a personal user account database for the application. Information stored locally by the application can generally be removed by deleting the relevant application data or uninstalling the application.

Information that may be processed by third-party service providers is subject to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or MusX, please contact the MusX development team through the support channel associated with the application.
